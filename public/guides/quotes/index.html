Buying

Read an alarm quote before you sign

Get the equipment list, total price and cancellation terms together in writing.

Separate equipment, installation and service

Ask for an itemized list of devices and a description of any installation, activation or support charges. Confirm which features are included and which require an ongoing plan. Use the same term when comparing offers.

Calculate the total for the full term

Add the one-time charges, the service payments for the term you would commit to, and any other required fees the provider discloses. Ask how taxes, price increases, annual fees or equipment financing affect that total.

Review changes and cancellation

Ask what happens if you move, the service stops meeting your needs or the introductory rate ends. Confirm how to cancel and which equipment you can keep using. Save a copy of the agreement and any promises written into it.

Verify the seller before giving access

Check the business identity using a number or website you find independently. The FTC warns that unsolicited sellers may misrepresent which company they work for or use a supposedly free system to sell a lengthy monitoring agreement.
