Monitoring

Compare alarm monitoring options

Ask who receives the first signal and what happens when that person cannot respond.

A local alarm

A local siren alerts someone within earshot. Ask whether the equipment also sends an app notification and which services are needed for that feature. Think about who can notice or respond while the home is vacant.

Self-monitoring

An app or message can notify authorized contacts. Write down who gets the first alert, who takes over when that person is unavailable and who can check on the property safely. Ask what notification behavior to expect during power or internet interruptions.

Professional monitoring

Ask the provider for its written response sequence, including how it tries to contact you, which events use verification and when a call to emergency services is possible. Local dispatch policies vary. Ask the provider to explain what applies at the service address.

Compare the same scenario

Ask each provider what happens if an alarm is triggered while you are travelling and your phone is switched off. Then compare the answer alongside the equipment ownership, support hours and complete monthly fee.
